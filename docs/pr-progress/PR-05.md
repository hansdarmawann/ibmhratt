### Real-data development estimate (seed 42, 30 trials per outer fold)

Training partition only. Not a final test, not comparable to the holdout table, and not evidence of generalization.

| Procedure | Outer AP (mean ± SD) | ROC-AUC | Precision | Recall | F1 | Selections | Thresholds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AutoML | 0.6529 ± 0.0553 | 0.8385 ± 0.0317 | 0.3818 ± 0.0441 | 0.7368 ± 0.0623 | 0.5000 ± 0.0367 | logistic regression 5/5 (C 0.092–0.201, unweighted) | 0.15–0.20 |
| Served baseline (fixed) | 0.6514 ± 0.0611 | 0.8392 ± 0.0289 | 0.3694 ± 0.0474 | 0.7579 ± 0.0653 | 0.4921 ± 0.0337 | logistic regression 5/5 (C 1.0) | 0.10–0.20 |

Paired AP difference (AutoML − baseline): mean +0.0014, SD 0.0074, range −0.0064 to +0.0138, AutoML higher in 3 of 5 folds. This is well within fold-to-fold variation: the two procedures are practically indistinguishable here. `automl_selection_optimism`: mean inner best-trial CV AP 0.6357 versus mean outer AP 0.6529 (difference −0.0172). The negative value reflects inner/outer sampling noise on small folds, not an absence of selection bias in general.
